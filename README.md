# Banking Business Analytics | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyses banking performance across customers, accounts, transactions, fraud, loans, customer service, and financial growth using Power BI.

The objective is to translate banking data into actionable business insights, identify performance gaps and risk indicators, and recommend strategies to improve customer engagement, fraud monitoring, credit quality, and operational performance.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse customer and account performance.
- Evaluate transaction values, volumes, and channel usage.
- Identify fraud patterns and credit-risk indicators.
- Assess loan portfolio performance and customer service efficiency.
- Monitor financial performance and growth using time intelligence.
- Develop actionable recommendations based on analytical findings.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Time Intelligence
- Data Visualisation
- Business Performance Analysis

## Dashboard Overview

### 1. Executive Overview

Analyses customer and account totals, transaction amounts, loan portfolio metrics, transaction channels, and geographic customer distribution.

### 2. Customer & Account Analytics

Examines account balances, active cards, customer income, credit scores, account status, customer acquisition trends, and occupation segments.

### 3. Transactions & Fraud Analytics

Evaluates transaction values, transaction volumes, fraudulent amounts, fraud rates, merchant categories, and fraud trends.

### 4. Loans & Customer Service Analytics

Analyses loan portfolio health, active and written-off loans, late payments, support tickets, and service resolution performance.

### 5. Financial Performance & Growth Analytics

Uses month-to-date (MTD), quarter-to-date (QTD), year-to-date (YTD), month-over-month (MoM), quarter-over-quarter (QoQ), year-over-year (YoY), cumulative totals, and moving averages to assess performance over time.

## Key Business Questions

- How effectively is the bank acquiring and engaging customers?
- Which account types contribute the most to total account balances?
- How do transaction values and volumes change over time?
- What is the scale of fraudulent transactions, and how does fraud vary across merchant categories?
- Which loan segments show signs of credit-quality problems?
- How efficiently are customer support tickets resolved?
- How does transaction performance compare year over year?
- Which channels and regions offer opportunities for business improvement?

## Selected Findings

The project presentation reports the following results:

- **Customer base:** Approximately 60,000 customers and 95,000 accounts.
- **Transaction value:** Approximately ₹1.42 billion for the analysed 2024 period.
- **Fraudulent amount:** Approximately ₹3.18 million, with a reported fraud rate of 0.49%.
- **Loan portfolio:** Approximately ₹9.21 billion across 22,000 loans.
- **Customer service:** Reported ticket resolution rate of 79.7%.
- **Year-over-year transaction growth:** Reported at 0.28%.

These figures reflect the results presented in the report. They should be interpreted in the context of the dataset, KPI definitions, and calculation assumptions.

## Business Recommendations

- Improve customer engagement and encourage appropriate cross-selling opportunities.
- Evaluate digital-channel adoption and opportunities to improve transaction convenience.
- Strengthen fraud monitoring using transaction patterns and merchant-category analysis.
- Improve credit-risk monitoring and introduce early-warning indicators for late payments.
- Reduce customer service delays, particularly for fraud- and KYC-related issues.
- Validate time-intelligence calculations before drawing conclusions about business growth.
